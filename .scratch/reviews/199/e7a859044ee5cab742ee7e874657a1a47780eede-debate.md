## Lane close-out on D1

**D1 is a false positive.** `query-string@9.5.1` strips a leading `?` unconditionally, regardless of `ignoreQueryPrefix` (which is not even a parse option in this version):

- `node_modules/query-string/base.js:392`: `query = query.trim().replace(/^[?#&]/, '');`
- README: `queryString.parse('?foo=bar') //=> {foo: 'bar'}`

Verified from the published package tarball (`query-string@9.5.1`). The example comment is correct; no change made.

D2 and D3 are notes, not defects. The base64 alphabet (`+`, `/`, `=`) is emitted verbatim, but the example's `parseSearch` reads the raw query string from `url.search` and calls `atob` on it — the router does not re-encode the `stringifySearch` output (`router.ts:1471,1493,2113`), so it round-trips. Left as-is; users wanting URL-escaped output are pointed at the `stringifySearchWith`/`parseSearchWith` helpers in the file's Context.
