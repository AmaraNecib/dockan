**P2 — hard, medium — the bounded backoff is cancelled by every request**
`main.ts:201` calls `reclaim?.trigger()` on *every* request, and `reclaim-retry.ts:96` (`this.cancelPending()`) cancels the pending automatic tick, re-arming it at the 15 s cap. While any client keeps asking, the automatic tick never fires and the attempt rate is the client's rate, not the schedule. Trigger: anything polling the refused port more often than 15 s. AC "retries the reclaim with bounded backoff while it serves the refusal" holds only while nobody asks; AC "the retry on the screen re-checks immediately" is met by hooking every request rather than the screen's retry. Fix: trigger on the explicit retry route and let a request-triggered attempt leave the schedule armed.
