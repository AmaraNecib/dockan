## Defender

- The two read shapes are deliberate and each is the right tool: `actAndAck` +
  eager read for a stay-put action (arm before the click; body read at headers),
  `readRpc` for a read around a navigation (no Playwright `Response` to lose).
  Collapsing them reintroduces one race or the other. The CI failure is the
  proof that the navigation case needs its own shape.
- The direct read is faithful: same `/api/rpc/<procedure>` endpoint, same
  `{ json }` envelope, same-origin credentials, and now the same vault header as
  the app link (`apps/web/src/lib/rpc.ts`).
- The eager read is still the correct shared default; all 12 `actAndAck` actions
  stay on the document and inherit the protection with no per-call boilerplate.
- The envelope check is lint-compliant, adds procedure-name attribution, and
  cannot change the success path (all procedures have non-void outputs).
- `rpcOutput`/`expectRpcOk` were dead after the helper change; deleting them
  removes the deferred-read seam rather than leaving a footgun.
- The vault header is inert in the shared suite (`DOKAN_VAULT=disabled`), so the
  change is a no-op there and forward-correct for a future vault-mode spec.

## Residual risks (honest)

1. `actAndAck` cannot detect a navigating action; the rule is documentation and
   review, not a runtime guard (a runtime guard would false-positive on SPA
   route changes).
2. `readRpc`'s non-2xx / malformed-envelope throws and the vault-header branch
   are not directly exercised by a focused helper test. The failures are loud,
   and the wisdom entry records the follow-up.
3. The `dokan.vault.session` key and `/api/rpc` prefix are duplicated in the
   test helper (unavoidable inside `page.evaluate`); drift fails loudly in vault
   mode and no-ops in disabled mode.

## Verdict

No open blocker. The class that produced the CI failure is removed at the shared
helper (eager read) and at its only navigating call sites (`readRpc`); the
review-driven fidelity and diagnostics gaps are closed; the residual items are
documented, deliberate, and out of round scope.
