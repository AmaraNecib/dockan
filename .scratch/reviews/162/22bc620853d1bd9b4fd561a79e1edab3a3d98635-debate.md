## Delta-review findings and disposition

- **Halted-path raw-submit bypass (critic 3/10, edge hunter RED).** The guard
  checked only `submitting || pay.isPending`; after a mid-loop failure `halted`
  blocked the button but a raw form submit would replan from stale rows and
  re-send money. **Fixed:** `halted` added to the in-`submit` guard, plus a
  raw-submit assertion in the halted test. This was the only correctness defect
  in the fix set.
- **Missing tests** for the settle invalid-percentage guard, the sale/purchase
  over-total guards, and the return value→0 percentage case. **Fixed** (see the
  table and the test-audit evidence).
- **Vacuous assertions** (`lastInput?.initialPaid` is `undefined` when a guard
  blocked the save). **Fixed:** `expect(lastInput).not.toBeNull()` added before
  the undefined checks.
- **Cancel/fields disabled only on `pay.isPending`.** **Fixed:** `|| submitting`
  added to the input, method select and Cancel.
- **UI shows `amount > reference` with `100%`** (over-reference amount, e.g. a
  pre-line draft larger than the first cart line). **Kept, documented:** the
  amount is the last-edited source and every consumer rejects the over-total
  amount before sending; changing the rounding would be a wider behaviour
  change. Not a money path.
- **Settle panel seeds once from `unpaid`.** **Dismissed:** the refetch that
  changes `unpaid` also unmounts the panel via `onDone`/`onCancel`; no reachable
  stale state.
- **Relink effect duplicated across sale/purchase/ReturnsPanel.** **Deferred**
  (judgement call): a shared `useLinkedAmount` hook is the right cleanup, but it
  would widen this review-fix diff; noted as a follow-up.

## Verdict

The one defect introduced by the first fix pass (the halted raw-submit bypass)
