## Verdict

The change closes every lane acceptance criterion with real assertions; the
audit found one high-risk gap (the sticky bar was never exercised under
scroll), two coverage holes on the teaching empty states, and several
robustness gaps in the new E2E harness. All high/medium findings are fixed in
this HEAD; the fixes were re-run through typecheck, lint, `bun run test`
(237 web tests), and `bun run test:e2e` (22/22).
