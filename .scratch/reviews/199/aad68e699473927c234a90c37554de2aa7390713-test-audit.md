# PR #199 — Delta test-audit at `aad68e6`

Auditor question: is the evidence sufficient for the claim that every fixed
example is correct, and what is not covered?

## What the evidence actually establishes

| Claim class | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|

## Gaps the auditor identified, and how they were closed

1. **`@tanstack/config` renames had no evidence** → closed by packing and reading
   the published typings (`tanstackConfig`, `publish`).
2. **Behavioural claims asserted, not proven** → closed by reading the installed
   package sources listed above; the `isLoading = isPending && isFetching`
   definition is in `queryObserver.ts`, and `preloadStaleTime`/`preloadGcTime`
   are separated in `load-client.ts`.
3. **The zero-price fix was itself unexercised** → closed by the live validator
   table (also caught the `NaN` regression, now fixed).
4. **Guards cannot compile markdown** → accepted and stated explicitly: guards
   prove the app is unaffected; the per-snippet `tsc` + round-trip runs are the
   evidence for the examples themselves.

## Residual, acknowledged limits

- `qs`, `jsurl2`, `query-string`, `@tanstack/zod-adapter`,
  `@tanstack/router-valibot-adapter`, and `@tanstack/config` are **not**
  dependencies of this repo. Their examples were verified against published
  packages (tarball typings / live runs), not against a build in this repo.
- `Route.id` vs `Route.fullPath` was verified from the type definitions and the
  generator's `inferFullPath` (pathless segments stripped). A generated-route
  tree is not present in this worktree, so it was not reproduced end to end.
- No automated test compiles the fenced examples; the typecheck of the final
  snippets was a scratch run, not a committed test.

## Verdict

Sufficient for a **docs-only** PR. The strong half (round-trips) and the
version-pinned type checks cover every fix whose correctness is mechanically
checkable; the remaining claims are backed by installed-package source. No
unverified fix ships as "proven"; the limits above are stated rather than
implied. The app-regression guards are green.
