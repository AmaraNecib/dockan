# Invoice list filters

The `/invoices` list filters business dates inclusively: a row dated on either entered endpoint remains in the result. With no explicit order, the UI and store preserve the historical newest-business-date-first query, with recording time as the same-date tie-break. The UI omits `order` for this default. Explicit OLDEST/NEWEST ordering also sorts by business date, then uses a consistent identifier category: numbered invoices come before unnumbered labels in OLDEST and after them in NEWEST. Numbered rows compare by numeric sequence; equal sequences, including an INV/SUP pair, use recording time and then invoice ID. Unnumbered legacy/custom labels use recording time and invoice ID. The explicit tie order does not change ledger recording order or any accounting result.

Invoice number bounds accept a bare sequence (`111`) or a generated number (`INV-00111`, `SUP-00111`). Bounds compare the numeric sequence across both prefixes: `INV-111` through `SUP-113` includes matching sales and purchases numbered 111–113. Generated numbers use `INV` for sales and `SUP` for purchases; four digits are a minimum, not a maximum. Legacy nonnumeric/custom invoice labels have no numeric sequence and are excluded whenever a number bound is active. A malformed or unsafe number, or a lower bound greater than its upper bound, is rejected.

The item-name/barcode/number contents search, kind, and payment-status filters compose with both ranges. Filtering and counting happen before pagination. Clear filters restores all kinds, statuses, ranges and newest-first ordering.
