## Residual risk

- The import's guard is read-window scoped; a write issued in the few-ms window
  after the check is not re-checked, but the legacy key is retained as a
  fallback.
- e2e runs the editable scanner regime at 250 ms; production stays at 50 ms and
  the unit test covers the slow-typing reset.
- The App test holds every `preferencesGet` to control the race; it does not
  model a hung write (the hook's write has no timeout, unchanged).
