## Remaining notes (accepted / deferred, no blocker)

- **e2e typo assertion is `toContainText` only.** Other `بطارية-*` items in the shared DB also fold to `بطاريه` and would fuzzy-match, so the step does not prove the fallback hit *this* spec's item. The exact behaviour is pinned at unit level (`catalog-search.test.ts`), and the article query has a negative control; a companion `not.toContainText` on the typo query is a nice-to-have.
- **Arabic-Indic digits** are not folded in name/category search (`foldArabic("زيت ٦٠")` stays `"زيت ٦٠"`); phone matching already folds them via `foldArabicDigits`. Not required by #165.
- **`الله` → `له`** is an accepted, pinned search-only over-fold; identity is untouched.
- **Threshold is length-sensitive** by construction (a 1-edit on 6 letters scores 0.833 and qualifies; on 5 letters 0.8 and does not). Documented in the constant and design doc.
- **Diacritic strip** covers `\u0640\u064B-\u0652\u0670` (same ranges as `normalizeTextKey`); `\u0653-\u0655` are composed by NFC so are not handled separately — low impact.
