  name at :584) — **no change**.

## Coverage (found)

- The reported race *is* closed for this spec: after the `itemsCreateWithStock` ack
  the only remaining writer of the disabled state is `createInvoice.isPending`, which
  can only flip *after* the click; a re-introduced block now fails at the
  `toBeEnabled` assert or the in-action `sale-error` assert (≤5s, named) instead of
  as an anonymous 30s wait.
- **Accepted as out of scope, handed to the parent**: eight sibling specs click the
  quick-add save and rely on a 5s UI poll with no ack (`sale-checkout`,
  `invoice-payments`, `returns`, `expense-reports`, `completion-screens`,
  `learnability`, `items-flow`). Extracting the ack into a shared helper and
  applying it suite-wide is a separate change; it is reported as a follow-up, not
  silently deferred.
- No regression test exists for the "click delivered while the button is disabled"
  class in general (it is a browser-level event drop, not app state). The mitigations
  (ack the only disabling writer + assert enabled) are the available guard; recorded
  as a residual risk rather than claimed as covered.

## Test quality (found)

- The three pre-click asserts are meaningful, not tautological: they assert state the
  test's own flow must produce (the created row, its value, its server-issued key,
  the enabled save). They cost ~0ms on a healthy run.
- The ack pattern matches the repo convention (arm before act, assert ok, read the
  envelope) and mirrors `invoice-print-a4.spec.ts:145`.
- The test's intent (the printed receipt) is unchanged; the added lines all serve
  step 2 of that narrative.
- Two comments were overclaiming (a mechanism the evidence does not support, and a
  "gate" that does not gate). Both were rewritten; the commit body was also
  rewritten to state the uncertainty honestly.

## Verdict
