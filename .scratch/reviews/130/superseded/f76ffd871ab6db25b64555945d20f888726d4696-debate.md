## Defender's case (kept, verified)

- Reusing the #165 fold adds **zero** new folding surface to review; the letters
  it folds beyond #130's list (ة→ه, ؤ→و) are the same kind of orthographic
  freedom, and tashkeel/tatweel/bidi stripping predates #130.
- Exact folded equality, never `fuzzySearchScore`: the fuzzy floor would make
  «محمد»/«محمود» warn, i.e. cry wolf on different names.
- The identity boundary holds: `normalizeTextKey`, `needsChoice`, storage and
  the store's unique phone are untouched, so the warning cannot merge balances.
- Non-blocking is structural: `createDisabled` never sees the duplicate warning,
  and the warning offers the existing record in one click.
