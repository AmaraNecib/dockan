## The finding and its structural fix

Round 3 added a folded-sibling signature scan to the explicit `supplierId` branch.
That re-targeted a user-chosen B to a sibling A holding an identical signature, so
two same-name suppliers blocked each other (the whole batch was refused).

Fixed by splitting the two cases at the source:

- **server**: the sibling signature scan applies only to `newSupplierName`. An
  explicit `supplierId` is never replaced. The name map is lazily built again.
- **client**: a row that originated from a typed name is sent as `newSupplierName`
  (a `namedTargets` map tracks the origin), never as a pre-resolved id.
