## Greptile-style depth checks (verified sound)

- Integer DZD math: sums/products of persisted ints only; `amount` capped at 1e12 (shared
  `MAX_EXPENSE_AMOUNT`); no floats.
- No double counting: `kind='SALE'` JOIN excludes purchases; discounts reduce revenue via
  post-allocation `netTotal` (Σ netTotal ≡ totalAmount by #30's allocation identity); withdrawals
  `continue` out of the operating bucket, so `byCategory` sums reconcile exactly.
- No stored totals: every figure recomputed at read time from raw rows; formulas change without
  touching history (backward compatibility).
- Pure module and SQL range predicates byte-consistent (`substr(...,1,10)` == `slice(0,10)`).
- Defender confirmed every design-doc decision is implemented as stated (accrual revenue,
  per-unit integer COGS per ADR-0005, append-only expenses enforced by absence of edit/delete
  routes, read-time derivation, rolling vs calendar windows, shared `resolveWindow`, byCategory
  identity). Four prose-ambiguity candidates resolved as non-defects.
