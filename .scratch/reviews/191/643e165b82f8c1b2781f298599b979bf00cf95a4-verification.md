## 5. Reproduction attempts (negative results, recorded)

- 60 instrumented `--workers=2` runs plus 60 more: symptom never reproduced.
- Pre-fix spec verbatim + CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 6 and 10: fails at an
  earlier `expect().toBeVisible()` (5s), **not** at the `invoicesCreate` ack. Uniform
  client-side slowness therefore does not produce the reported signature — which also
  means a naive "CI is slower" story is insufficient, and is why the commit body does
  not claim a mechanism.
- The one local pre-fix failure (1 of 14 sequential runs) was not captured with
  artifact detail; it is not counted as evidence in either direction.

## 6. What the evidence rules out

- A client-side refusal of the save: `sale-error` empty in every measured run, and no
  refusal path can trigger with a resolved cart line and paid 500 of total 900.
- An optimistic line with an unresolved item id: the line is added only from the
  `itemsCreateWithStock` response and the cart row is keyed on the id it returns.
- Server-side slowness: `invoicesCreate` answered in 92–135ms, including at the end
  of a 56-test run against the accumulated e2e database.
- A uniformly slower CI: 5.3m (CI) vs 5.1–5.2m (local) for the same suite.

## 7. Residual uncertainty (for the parent)

A `page.waitForResponse` timeout is emitted by whichever wait is outstanding when the
30s test budget dies; it does not prove a request was never sent, and no artifact from
`gh run 38464745468` survives (the e2e job uploads none). The remaining candidates are
budget exhaustion across the pre-save phase or a one-off transport stall. The fix
addresses what is provable — the un-acked write dependency and the unattributable
failure — so a recurrence now fails by name instead of anonymously.
