## Defender's case (accepted)

FIFO snapshots + weighted-average live cache is not an ADR conflict ("past
sales keep theirs" mandates per-sale snapshots; outflows never move the live
average — proven by a test asserting costAtSale 200 while `items.costPrice`
stays 150). Largest-remainder allocation reconciles exactly; serialized units
+ atomic `UPDATE…RETURNING` numbering; additive columns with nullish fallbacks.
