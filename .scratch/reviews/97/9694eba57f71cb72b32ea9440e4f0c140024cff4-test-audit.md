## Residual risk

- Reviewers could not execute the suites (read-only); the parent ran the green
  gates above at this HEAD.
- `--repeat-each` isolation is per repeat group (`beforeAll`); it does not
  support multiple concurrent workers on the shared database.
- The fresh-install E2E remains order-sensitive for *which spec runs first*
  (`00-` prefix + `workers: 1`); the reset removes its dependence on prior
  groups' data.
