### Additional defects found during synthesis (not raised by the critic)

- **Same-second ordering was non-deterministic.** `createdAt` has second
  precision, so two invoices created in the same second fell through to a random
  UUID. `distributePayment` now tie-breaks on the invoice number's numeric
  sequence before the id. Caught by the e2e (the preview settled the newer
  invoice first). Fixed and unit-tested.
- **A draft typed before the first cart line was wiped.** The `total === 0`
  effect branch cleared and reset the source on every run, so a payment typed
  while the cart was empty was lost when the first line landed. Caught by
  `receipts.spec.ts`. Fixed: an edited draft survives; only an untouched pair
  follows the empty cart. Unit-tested.
